OTT Money Saver

Privacy Policy  |  Effective Date: June 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to OTT Money Saver. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. Please read it carefully.

By using OTT Money Saver, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Full name and nickname
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • UPI ID or bank account details (for withdrawals only)

Transaction Information

  • Payment screenshots uploaded for verification
  • UPI transaction reference numbers
  • Wallet balance and transaction history
  • Screen purchase and sale records

Usage Information

  • Chat messages between buyers and sellers
  • Ratings and reviews you submit
  • OTT platform group details created by sellers
  • Device type and operating system

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Facilitate screen sharing transactions between buyers and sellers
  • Process and verify payments through admin approval
  • Manage your wallet — locked and unlocked amounts
  • Enable real-time chat between buyers and sellers
  • Send important notifications about your account and transactions
  • Improve our app and provide customer support
  • Prevent fraud and ensure platform safety

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share limited information in the following cases:

  • Between buyers and sellers: Your nickname and unique ID are visible to the other party in a transaction for communication purposes
  • Admin team: Our admin team reviews payment screenshots to verify transactions
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights
Your full name, mobile number, email, and UPI details are never shared publicly or with other users.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase — a trusted cloud database platform with industry-standard security.

  • All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS
  • Database access is protected with Row Level Security (RLS)
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • Payment screenshots are stored in secure private storage

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

6. Chat Messages

Chat messages between buyers and sellers are stored to:

  • Resolve disputes between parties
  • Ensure platform safety and prevent misuse
  • Provide customer support when needed

Messages are only accessible by the two parties involved and our admin team for moderation purposes.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction — Update or correct your profile information
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdrawal — Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

8. Children's Privacy

OTT Money Saver is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

If we discover that a child under 18 has provided personal information, we will delete it immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
