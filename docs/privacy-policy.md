# Privacy Policy — ShareOTTs

**Last updated: 10 August 2026**

ShareOTTs ("we", "us", "the app") lets people share the cost of OTT
subscriptions with one another. This policy explains what we collect, why, and
what you can do about it.

If anything here is unclear, write to **shareottssupport@gmail.com**.

---

## 1. Who we are

ShareOTTs is operated by Maakam Bharath Kumar, India.

Contact: shareottssupport@gmail.com

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Netflix, Amazon Prime
Video, Disney+ Hotstar, ZEE5, Sony LIV or any other streaming service named in
the app. Those names are used only to describe what a listing is for.

---

## 2. What we collect

**When you create an account**
- Your name and nickname
- Your mobile number
- Your email address, if you give one
- A profile photo, if you upload one
- Your password, stored only as an encrypted hash — we never see it

**To pay you**
- Account holder name, UPI ID, bank account number and IFSC code

We collect these only so we can send you money you have withdrawn. They are
shown to nobody else.

**When you use the app**
- Groups you join or create
- Payments, top-ups, withdrawals and refunds
- Messages and photos you send in chat
- Ratings and badges you give or receive
- Whether the app is currently open, so others see you as online

**Automatically**
- A device token, if you allow notifications
- Basic error information when something goes wrong

**We do not collect** your location, your contacts, your photo library beyond
the images you deliberately upload, or anything from other apps.

---

## 3. Why we collect it

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Name, mobile, password | To create and secure your account |
| Bank and UPI details | To pay out money you withdraw |
| Payment records | To keep your wallet accurate, and because we are required to keep financial records |
| Chat messages | So buyers and sellers can arrange access |
| Ratings and badges | So other users can judge who to deal with |
| Device token | To send you notifications you asked for |

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising.

---

## 4. Who can see your information

**Other users** see only your name, your user ID number, your rating, your
badges, and whether you are online. They never see your mobile number, email,
bank details or password.

**We can see** your account details, your transactions, and your chat messages.
Chat is not end-to-end encrypted. We may read conversations to resolve a
dispute, investigate fraud, or where the law requires it. Please do not share
passwords, OTPs or personal contact details in chat — the app warns you about
this each time.

**Our service providers** process data on our behalf:
- **Supabase** — database and file storage
- **Cashfree Payments** — payment processing
- **Google Firebase** — notifications

Each has its own privacy policy and handles data only as instructed by us.

**Legal disclosure** — we will share information where we are legally required
to, or to protect against fraud.

---

## 5. Payments

Card and bank details entered during payment go directly to Cashfree. **We never
see or store your card number.** We keep only the amount, the date, and a
reference number.

The bank details you save for withdrawals are stored by us, and can only be
changed after we manually verify the request. This is deliberate: it stops
someone who has stolen your password from redirecting your money.

---

## 6. How long we keep it

- **Account information** — while your account exists
- **Financial records** — up to 8 years after the transaction, as Indian tax law requires
- **Chat messages** — while the related group is active, then up to 12 months
- **Uploaded images** — up to 12 months after the related group ends

When you delete your account we remove your profile, chats and images. We keep
financial records for the period above, because we are legally obliged to.

---

## 7. Your rights

You can:
- **See** the information we hold about you
- **Correct** anything that is wrong
- **Delete** your account and your data, subject to the financial records above
- **Withdraw** notification permission at any time, in your phone settings
- **Object** to how we use your data

Write to **shareottssupport@gmail.com** and we will respond within 30 days.

Before we can delete an account, any money in your wallet must be withdrawn and
any active group memberships must have ended.

---

## 8. Security

Your password is hashed, never stored in readable form. All traffic between the
app and our servers is encrypted. Access to the database is restricted so one
user cannot read another user's records.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you.

---

## 9. Children

ShareOTTs is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information
from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we
will remove it.

---

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and notify you in
the app. Continuing to use ShareOTTs after a change means you accept it.
